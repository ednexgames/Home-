Privacy Policy

Issued by Ednex Game Studio · Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Ednex Game Studio (“Ednex,” “we,” or “us”) may collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile games, apps, or related services (“Services”). The information involved depends on the particular game, its features, and the permissions you choose to grant. Please review this policy when you use our Services. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and provide additional notice where appropriate.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the Services you use, information may be collected directly from you, automatically from your device, or through a platform or service you connect to a game.

Device and activity information

This may include device type and manufacturer, operating system, language and time zone, IP address, approximate region, app version, access times, crash reports, and other diagnostic or log data.

Identifiers and gameplay information

Games may process app or device identifiers, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, gameplay progress, scores, achievements, settings, and interactions with game features or other players.

Account and transaction information

If a game supports sign-in, social features, or purchases, we may receive information such as a player name or platform identifier, account details made available by that platform, purchase records, and virtual-item history. Payment details are generally handled by the relevant app store or payment provider rather than collected by us.

Permissions and third-party sources

Some features may request device permissions or receive information from services you choose to connect, such as Google Play Games or Apple Game Center. Any access depends on the feature, platform settings, and your authorization. You can review and change permissions in your device settings.

2. How we use information

We may use information, as applicable, to:

  • operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Services;
  • save progress and provide requested game features, support, or purchases;
  • understand performance and improve gameplay, design, and reliability;
  • remember settings and personalize parts of the experience;
  • detect fraud, abuse, technical issues, or activity that violates our terms; and
  • meet legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of users and Ednex.

Where required by law, we rely on an applicable legal basis, which may include providing a service you request, our legitimate interests, your consent, or compliance with a legal obligation. If a game offers optional marketing or personalized advertising, the relevant choices will be presented through the game, your device, or the applicable platform.

3. How information may be shared

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Information may be shared, where relevant to a Service, with:

  • vendors that provide hosting, analytics, crash reporting, customer support, advertising, or other services on our behalf;
  • platforms you use to sign in, play, or make purchases, under their own terms and privacy notices;
  • other players, when you use a feature that makes gameplay or profile information visible;
  • authorities or other parties when required by law or needed to protect people, rights, or the Services; and
  • a successor or buyer in connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable privacy protections.

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Their practices and the features available can differ between games and regions.

4. Advertising and analytics

Some Services may use analytics or advertising providers to understand app performance or show ads. Depending on the game and your choices, these providers may process device or advertising identifiers, app activity, and general location information. You can manage available advertising and tracking choices through your device settings and any controls presented in the game. Consult the privacy information for the specific app and its providers for details that apply to you.

For details about third-party platforms and providers that may be relevant to a game, review their privacy resources:

  • Facebook
  • Apple Game Center
  • Google
  • AppLovin
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity
  • Vungle
  • Pangle
  • Mintegral
  • Firebase

These links are provided as reference resources; the providers used may vary by game.

5. Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including to provide the Services, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary by data type and service. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children’s privacy

Our Services are not intended to collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required unless the applicable requirements are met. The age rules and available features may vary by game and region. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us through the developer contact details on the relevant app-store listing so we can review the request.

7. Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict some processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. To make a request, use the developer contact details shown on the relevant app-store listing and tell us which game and region your request concerns. We may need to verify your request and may be unable to fulfill it in some circumstances permitted by law.

You can also manage app permissions, advertising settings, and connected accounts using your device or platform controls.

8. International processing and third-party services

Ednex and its service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers. Our games may link to third-party services; we do not control their content or privacy practices, so please review their notices before using them.

9. Changes and contact

We may revise this policy as our Services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Ednex Game Studio using the developer contact details provided on the relevant app-store listing.
